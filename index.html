Analysis Framework

How We Analyze Any Business

Every successful product starts by answering these five critical questions.

01

Who is the Client?

Identify who pays for the solution and what success means to them.

Example: Damascus Restaurant Owner
  • Increase monthly orders
  • Improve customer retention
  • Grow revenue
02

Who is the User?

Understand the people who will actually use the product.

Example: Restaurant Customers
  • Looking for authentic Syrian food
  • Want fast service
  • Need easy online ordering
03

What is the Problem?

Discover the real pain point before building anything.

Example: Low visibility online
  • Few website visitors
  • Poor search ranking
  • Low conversion rate
04

How Does It Create Value?

Define how the product improves users' lives.

Example: Authentic dining experience
  • Traditional Syrian recipes
  • Unique menu items
  • Family-friendly atmosphere
05
Final Step

How Does It Make Money?

Understand the business model and revenue stream.

Example: Revenue Sources
  • Dine-in customers
  • Food delivery services
  • Catering orders
